GOOGLE PLAY & GLOBAL COMPLIANCE

PRIVACY POLICY

Developer: AetherForge Interactive · Application ID: com.aetherforge.unboundsuns · Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Information We Process

  • Account & Gameplay Data: Pilot username, PBKDF2-hashed credentials, unique account identifier, ship loadouts, currencies (Silver, Gold, Sovereign Seals), crafting and quest progress, player market orders, guild vault records, and real-time multiplayer telemetry stored on our authoritative game database strictly to operate the persistent online universe.
  • Anti-Cheat & Fair Play Security Logs: Authoritative UDP session tickets, connection anomaly flags (e.g., speed/damage tamper alerts), and failed authentication telemetry used strictly to protect game integrity and prevent exploits.
  • In-App Purchases & Official Tebex Web Shop Billing: Purchase tokens, product IDs, and order IDs verified via Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) or our independent international Web Shop Merchant of Record, Tebex Limited (POST /api/billing/tebex/webhook). AetherForge Interactive never receives or stores credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank credentials.
  • Optional Bug Reports & Support Dispatches: Messages, pilot IDs, and sector identifiers voluntarily submitted through the in-game Feedback panel (/api/feedback) or our Support Center.
  • Advertising & Analytics Identifiers (Mobile): On Android builds, the Google Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) may be processed by the integrated Unity Ads SDK for optional rewarded ads, frequency capping, and ad fraud prevention in accordance with Google Play policies.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

All economy mutations are validated server-side with double-entry transactional integrity checks. Passwords are stored using 100,000-iteration PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with unique cryptographic salts. We do not sell personal contact data, player rosters, or billing profiles to third-party data brokers.

3. Data Retention

Gameplay and economy records are retained while your Pilot Account remains active so your fleet and Citadel assets persist across sessions and platforms. Minimal anonymized billing transaction hashes and anti-fraud audit records may be retained as required by applicable tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention laws.

4. Account & Data Deletion Request (7-Day SLA)

You may request permanent deletion of your Unbound Suns Pilot Account, credentials, synced inventory/economy records, and optional bug report entries at any time using either method:

  • In-Game: Open the Citadel Support / Feedback panel and submit a Data Deletion request including your Pilot Username or Account ID.
  • Via Web Portal: Submit a Data Deletion Request through our Support Center or contact our developer email listed on the Google Play store page.

All verified deletion requests are permanently fulfilled within 7 calendar days. Once completed, account deletion is irreversible and all virtual ships, Silver, Gold, and Sovereign Seals associated with the account are permanently erased.

5. Children's Privacy

Unbound Suns is rated for audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal contact information from children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has submitted personal data, contact us immediately for prompt removal.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as our platform expands. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy inquiries, reach out via our Support Center.
